FAQ
What is HeraHealth?

HeraHealth is a women’s health awareness prototype for symptom journaling, basic record-based insights and preparing for healthcare conversations.

Can HeraHealth diagnose a condition?

No. It does not diagnose, predict disease or replace a clinician. Its insights describe the entries you provide. For concerning or worsening symptoms, contact a qualified healthcare professional; seek urgent local care for emergencies.

Are my records medical advice?

No. The app organises self-reported notes and general educational information. It may not capture every relevant detail, so review your summary and discuss concerns with a clinician.

Should I enter real medical information in this demo?

Please use fictional demonstration data. This hackathon prototype is not a certified medical record system and has not undergone a security audit.
