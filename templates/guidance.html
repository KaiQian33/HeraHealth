HEALTH AWARENESS

Health Guidance

Use these general prompts to prepare for a health conversation.

Important: This page is general educational guidance, not personalised medical advice. It does not diagnose conditions.
01

Keep useful notes

Record when a symptom started, how long it lasted, how severe it felt, what affected daily life and any questions you want to remember.

02

Prepare for appointments

Bring your timeline, current medicines or supplements if relevant, and questions about symptoms, possible next steps and follow-up.

03

Know when to seek help

If symptoms are persistent, worsening or concerning, contact a qualified healthcare professional. For severe or rapidly worsening symptoms or an emergency, seek urgent local medical care.

When to seek urgent medical help

Safety information, not a diagnosis.

If you have severe or rapidly worsening symptoms, do not wait for an app response. Seek urgent medical care. Examples of emergency warning signs can include:

  • Difficulty breathing, collapse, or loss of consciousness.
  • Severe chest pain or sudden confusion, weakness, or difficulty speaking.
  • Heavy bleeding, especially with dizziness, faintness, or severe pain.
  • Severe or sudden abdominal or pelvic pain, particularly if pregnancy is possible.
  • Any symptom that feels life-threatening or is rapidly getting worse.
If this may be an emergency: contact your local emergency number or go to the nearest emergency department. In Malaysia, call 999. Do not use HeraHealth or the Health Assistant to decide whether an emergency is safe to wait out.

This list is not exhaustive. Warning signs and appropriate care can depend on the person and circumstances. Content should be reviewed by a qualified healthcare professional before public clinical use.

Reliable places to learn more

For information, start with recognised public health services and professional medical organisations. Check the date and source of any health information you read.
